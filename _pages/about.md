I obtained my Ph.D. degree at the AI Thrust of the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I am fortunate and horned to be supervised by [Professor Hui Xiong](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/people/hui-xiong/). I completed a visiting student program at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (CWB) from Aug. 2024 to Jan. 2025, under the guidance of [Professor James Kwok](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~jamesk/).

I hold a Master's degree from the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University, and a Bachelor's degree from the School of Physics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

My research interests include knowledge distillation, domain adaptation, multi-modal learning, large language models, and medical image analysis.
